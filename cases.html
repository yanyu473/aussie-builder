Case studies

Five industries, and what changes between them

Same engine, different builds. A restaurant needs bookings and a menu; a hardware shop needs a catalogue and a store locator. Open any card for the full screenshot and what's inside.

All case studies

Restaurant & café

Bistro Nord

Menu, bookings and trading hours on one screen.

Sydney NSW5 pages

A single-page restaurant site with tonight's menu on the homepage — a PDF menu is the first thing people give up on. Bookings run through the owner's existing OpenTable widget; we didn't rebuild what already worked.

No online ordering. The kitchen takes orders by phone and wants to keep it that way.

  • 5 pages
  • Menu + bookings
  • Google Maps
  • Contact form
Nail & beauty

Fern & File

Service menu, online booking, Afterpay explained.

Melbourne VIC4 pages

Price list above the fold, because that's what people came for. Booking is a Fresha embed. Afterpay and gift cards sit on their own page so the homepage stays quiet.

No e-commerce. Products are sold in the chair, not shipped from a warehouse.

  • 4 pages
  • Price list
  • Fresha booking
  • Instagram feed
Hardware & trade

Rivet & Co.

Product catalogue, stock check, trade account signup.

Brisbane QLD8 pages

Built around a trade counter, not a shopping cart. Twelve thousand lines are searchable, but checkout is a phone call — that's how this shop actually sells. The trade account form drops straight into their inbox.

No checkout. Trade customers ring the counter — a cart would have been decoration.

  • 8 pages
  • Catalogue search
  • Trade account form
  • Store locator
Sports store

Coastline Sports

Category navigation, free gait-fit booking, store info.

Perth WA6 pages

Five categories, one page each. The gait fit is the point — it's what separates them from the big-box stores, so it gets its own booking form and a Saturday slot.

No membership portal. Club sign-ups still run through the clubs themselves.

  • 6 pages
  • Category pages
  • Gait-fit booking
  • Click & collect
Golf retail

Fairway Golf

Custom fitting, pre-owned trade-in, member benefits.

Adelaide SA7 pages

A premium shop needs restraint: four brands, generous whitespace, and the fitting service up front. The pre-owned section is a plain table the owner updates herself.

No tee-time booking. That lives in the course's own system, not the shop's site.

  • 7 pages
  • Fitting booking
  • Pre-owned listing
  • Member page
