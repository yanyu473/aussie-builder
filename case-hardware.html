Rivet & Co.

A trade counter with twelve thousand lines and no intention of becoming an online shop.

Brisbane QLD 8 pages Custom 12 business days

The brief

Rivet & Co. sells to tradespeople who ring the counter, and they wanted to keep it that way. What they didn't have was a way for a new customer to check whether they stocked a particular fastener before driving to Virginia.

The brief was a catalogue you can search, a store locator, and a trade account form that lands in the right inbox. Nothing that takes an order.

What we built

  • Searchable catalogueTwelve thousand lines, filterable by trade and by brand.
  • Trade account formName, ABN, two referees. Goes straight to the accounts inbox.
  • Store locator and hoursBy day, including the 6am trade-counter start.
  • No cart, anywhereCheckout is a phone call. We didn't pretend otherwise.

What we left out

No checkout. Trade customers ring the counter — a cart would have been decoration.
