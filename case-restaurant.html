Bistro Nord

A 32-seat room in Neutral Bay that was tired of answering the same three questions on the phone.

Sydney NSW 5 pages Professional 7 business days

The brief

Bistro Nord had a menu PDF from 2019 floating around, a Facebook page nobody updated, and a phone that rang during service. The owner wanted three things answered before anyone walked in: what's on tonight, when are you open, and can I get a table.

They already paid for OpenTable and were happy with it. The site's job was to sit in front of that, not replace it.

What we built

  • Tonight's menu, on the homepagePulled from a plain text file the chef edits each morning. No PDF anywhere.
  • Bookings, handed offThe OpenTable widget opens in a panel. We didn't rebuild what already worked.
  • Trading hours, stated twiceOnce in the header, once beside the map — because that's the actual question.
  • One page, five anchorsNobody reads a restaurant's About page. We didn't build one.

What we left out

No online ordering. The kitchen takes orders by phone and wants to keep it that way.
