How it works

What "AI-built website" actually means here

There are two kinds of AI website tool. One hands you a drag-and-drop editor and wishes you luck. The other builds the thing first and shows you. We're the second kind.

01Process

Four steps, and you see something at every one

You

Step 1 — the questionnaire

Ten minutes, about fifteen questions. Industry, location, trading hours, what you want to be known for, whether you already have photos. Skip what you don't know — we'll chase it.

We do

Step 2 — AI drafts the site

An industry template sets the structure. Your answers become the copy. Colour and type come from a set of palettes tuned for Australian local business. What comes back is a clickable draft, not a mockup.

You

Step 3 — you review it

You get a preview link that works on a phone and a desktop. Mark up what's wrong directly on the page, or send us a list. Starter includes one revision round, Professional three.

We do

Step 4 — deliver and publish

Domain, business email, Google indexing, analytics. On launch day you get a written guide to editing text and swapping photos yourself.

02What's included

The baseline, on every package

None of this is an add-on. It's the floor.

Mobile-first layout

Laid out at 375px first, then scaled up. Not a desktop design squeezed down.

Contact and enquiry forms

With a honeypot and rate limiting, so most of the junk never reaches you.

Google Maps and trading hours

Location, opening hours and tap-to-call, where customers actually look for them.

Basic SEO structure

Titles, descriptions, structured data, sitemap. Keyword work starts at Professional.

HTTPS and Australian hosting

Sydney edge by default, so local visitors aren't waiting on a server in another hemisphere.

A written handover guide

Delivered on launch day. How to change text, swap photos, read your traffic numbers.

03On launch day

What actually lands in your hands

Not a promise — the literal list of what you get handed when the site goes live.

  • Domain, pointed and liveRegistered in your name, DNS configured, HTTPS enforced.
  • The site filesStatic files you own outright. No proprietary platform to rent back.
  • An admin loginChange text, swap photos, edit prices — without calling us.
  • A written handover guideHow to do the three things you'll actually do, with screenshots.
  • Analytics, already wiredGA4 or Plausible connected, with the events that matter firing.
  • Search Console set upSitemap submitted, indexing requested, property verified.
  • Business email workinghello@yourdomain, set up, tested, and forwarding where you want it.
  • A speed reportLighthouse scores on mobile and desktop, handed over with the site.
04What we don't do

Out of scope, said plainly

Better to draw the line now than argue about it later.

  • Online payments and e-commerce checkout
  • Customer logins, memberships, self-service dashboards
  • Booking and scheduling engines (we can embed a third-party one)
  • Multi-language site clusters
  • Ad campaigns and ongoing content marketing
