Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026.

This policy explains what Aussie Builder Pty Ltd (ABN 12 345 678 901) collects when you use this website or enquire about a project, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. It's written to sit alongside the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

Only what you type into the forms, plus basic technical data from your visit:

  • Enquiry details — business name, industry, your name, email, phone, current website, project description and the package you're interested in.
  • Chat transcripts — what you type into the assistant, including any contact details you choose to leave there.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, approximate location (city level), device type, referring site. Collected through analytics, in aggregate.
  • Cookies — a language preference cookie, plus analytics cookies if you accept them.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To deliver a project if you engage us.
  • To understand which pages are useful, so we can fix the ones that aren't.
  • To meet our record-keeping obligations.

We don't sell your details, and we don't add you to a marketing list because you asked for a quote.

Who else sees it

We use a small number of service providers to run the business — website hosting, email, form delivery and analytics. They process data on our instructions and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. If any of them store data outside Australia, we take reasonable steps to ensure the standard of protection is comparable to the APPs.

We will disclose information if we're legally required to, and not otherwise.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't turn into work are deleted after 24 months. Project records are kept for seven years, which is how long tax and corporate records need to be retained. Chat transcripts are deleted after 12 months.

Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies keep your language preference and stop forms being submitted twice. Analytics cookies are optional and only set if you accept them. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings; the site keeps working.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete what we're not required to keep;
  • stop using your details for a particular purpose.

Email privacy@aussiebuilder.com.au and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're unhappy with the response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Terms of service

Quotes and pricing

Prices on this site are starting prices in Australian dollars, excluding GST. A quote is only binding once it's issued in writing and accepted by both parties. Anything not listed in that quote is out of scope.

Payment

50% deposit on acceptance, balance on delivery. Invoices are payable within 14 days by bank transfer. Work begins once the deposit clears.

What we need from you

Content, logos and photos, plus timely feedback. Delivery timelines assume you return content and revision notes within five business days. If a project stalls for more than 60 days waiting on you, we may re-quote.

Revisions

Each package includes a set number of revision rounds, defined as one consolidated list of changes. Additional rounds are billed hourly.

Ownership

On final payment, you own the site content and the design as delivered. We retain the right to show the work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to in writing. We keep the underlying templates and tooling.

Liability

We'll fix defects in what we built. We're not liable for losses caused by third-party services, changes you make after handover, or search rankings — nobody can guarantee those.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of New South Wales, Australia.
