06FAQ

The four questions we get every week

How long does it take?
First draft in 3 business days. Full delivery: 5 business days on Starter, 7 on Professional, 10–14 on Custom. The clock starts the day you send your content, not the day you sign.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. Your job is to explain the business — what you sell, who buys it, what you want them to do. Layout and code are ours.
Can I update content myself later?
Yes. You get an admin login at launch — change text, swap photos, adjust prices. If you'd rather not, we do updates on a per-job basis.
What do I need to provide?
A logo (we can make a wordmark if you don't have one), shopfront or product photos, your trading hours and address, and a few lines about the business. Can't write the copy? Answer the questionnaire plainly and we'll write it.
